Fish sources and game adaptations / 鱼类素材及改动

Patrolling Pollock (PW2603MR0069, 68 cm), rockside Haddock
(HW2603MR0074, 48.5 cm), and Atlantic cod (CW2603MR0056, 70.5 cm,
Borgundfjorden specimen) are scans of actual marine fish specimens, not live
underwater scans. Source: FishSim — A Comprehensive Dataset of Fish Geometry,
Biomechanics and Contact Friction for Fish Processing Simulation; NTNU via
DataverseNO, https://doi.org/10.18710/OY9QWA
CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Sardina pilchardus (European sardine) and Scomber scombrus (Atlantic
mackerel) are realistic models of their species, not verified scans:
World Fish GLB, CodePawl; made with the fish-kit method by @nachat_dayo.
https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/world-fish-glb
https://huggingface.co/datasets/codepawl/world-fish-glb/blob/main/LICENSES.md
CC0 1.0.

Modified in this game: reduced geometry, texture compressed, normalized
orientation and scale, instance animation, underwater color response,
rendered fallback sprites.
